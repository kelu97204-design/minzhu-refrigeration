Photographs represent catalogue series and historic factory environments. No exact SKU match or present-day factory condition is inferred from them.

Runtime uses native width/height; the photo frame reserves height, and images load eagerly for mobile WebKit. Manual swipe gallery retains the previous photo until the selected next image loads. No player controls or autoplay. Photo opacity is 0.86. Factory images use proportional fill; the first factory photo is anchored to the top so the rooftop company name stays visible. Product and certificate images use contain to preserve complete equipment and the certificate grid.

The courtyard slide has been removed from the gallery; its original extracted asset is retained. Honours and certificates from slide 3 occupy the final gallery slide. The certificate caption identifies the catalogue source and makes no claim about current certification validity.
